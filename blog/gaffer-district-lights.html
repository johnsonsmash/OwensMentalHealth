Behind the Shot: Gaffer District lights after rain

The gear, the settings, and the lesson: repetition + attention can be regulation. Photography is exposure therapy for overwhelm—one frame at a time.

Gear & settings (for the curious)

  • Camera: phone or mirrorless—what matters is that you actually take the shot.
  • Shutter: ~1/30s handheld; brace on a post if you’re slower.
  • ISO: as low as you can get away with; noise isn’t the enemy, perfectionism is.

What this has to do with mental health

Showing up for a small creative ritual turns down threat and turns up agency. It’s not treatment—but it’s part of a treatment life.

Try this

  1. Pick one block. Photograph it in three kinds of weather this month.
  2. One sentence per photo about how the scene felt. That’s your check‑in.
  3. Share with a friend or keep it private—either way, you showed up.
