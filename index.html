New Patients

A simple checklist to get you started. No PHI through the website—once we connect, we’ll send secure intake forms.

Before Your First Visit

  • Complete our secure intake packet (sent via HIPAA‑compliant link after you contact us).
  • Have a list of current medications, doses, and allergies.
  • Upload prior records (if available): recent notes, labs, or testing.
  • Choose a quiet, private space for telehealth; test camera, mic, and internet.
  • Have your photo ID and insurance card (front/back) ready.
  • For minors: a parent/guardian must attend the first visit and consent to treatment.

What We Treat

Evaluation and medication management across the lifespan (children, adolescents, adults):

  • ADHD, anxiety disorders, depression
  • Trauma‑related conditions
  • Sleep concerns and mood regulation

We coordinate with therapists and primary care. Higher‑acuity needs may be referred to specialized services for safety and continuity.

Urgent Safety Note: If you are experiencing thoughts of harming yourself or others, or a medical/psychiatric emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. You may also call/text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Practice Policies

Scheduling & Cancellations

  • Please give 24 business hours notice to cancel or reschedule.
  • No‑show/late‑cancel fees may apply to self‑pay and out‑of‑network care.
  • Telehealth: join 5 minutes early; after 10 minutes late, the visit may be rescheduled.

Medication & Refills

  • Refills are processed during business hours with up to 2 business days turnaround.
  • Controlled substances require active follow‑up care and monitoring; early refills cannot be guaranteed.
  • Please request refills through your pharmacy or patient portal (when enabled).

Communication

  • Non‑urgent questions are answered within 1–2 business days.
  • Clinical questions may require a scheduled visit for safe care.
  • Do not use email for emergencies or PHI; use the portal once active.

Billing & Insurance

  • We are credentialing with NY plans; until confirmed, we provide superbills for out‑of‑network reimbursement.
  • HSA/FSA cards accepted; payment due at time of service.
  • Prior authorizations are completed when clinically appropriate.

Informed Consent & Privacy

We provide consent and privacy documents electronically before your first visit. Telehealth services are delivered via HIPAA‑compliant platforms. We follow federal and New York State regulations for confidentiality and record keeping.

Crisis Resources: Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), dial 911, or go to the nearest emergency department for urgent concerns.

FAQ

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Most visits are telehealth across NY with secure, HIPAA‑compliant video. In‑person visits are limited and scheduled as needed.

Do you provide therapy?

Medication management is primary. We partner with local therapists and can coordinate referrals.

Do you prescribe controlled substances?

When clinically appropriate and in alignment with NY and federal guidelines. We use treatment agreements and monitoring.
